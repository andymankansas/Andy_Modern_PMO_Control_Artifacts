# Walking Deck Interview - Question Bank

The agent uses these questions to gather the content for each slide block. It
asks one topic at a time, reuses anything already learned from artifacts, and
helps you distill each answer to a single sentence that leads with the "why."
Every block is optional - skip any that your deck does not need.

Guiding rule for every slide: **one key message, in plain language, tied to the
business objective.** The reader may know nothing about the project and has
10-20 minutes.

---

## 1. Audience & objective  → `meta`, `cover`, `agenda`
- Who is the primary audience, and what do they care about?
- In one sentence, what is the objective of this deck?
- What is the project or program called? Any subtitle or tagline?
- What date or version stamp should appear on the cover?

## 2. Business context  → `problem.context`
- Which organization or team do we serve, and who do they roll up to?
- What are their business objectives and strategy?
- What does success look like for them?

## 3. Problem / why change  → `problem`
- What is broken or costly today? (List 2-4 concrete pain points.)
- What is the cost of leaving it unmitigated (cost, risk, speed, trust)?
- Group the pain into 2-3 themes (e.g. fragmented intake, data drift, impact).

## 4. Solution / operating model  → `vision`
- In one sentence, what is the solution - and **why** does it matter to the business?
- What are the 3-4 steps of the operating model, in order?
- What is the single "so what" (cost, trust, speed) the audience should remember?

## 5. How it works  → `how_it_works`
- What are the stages of the lifecycle or process (up to 9), in order?
- For each stage, a 3-5 word description.
- Why does this help governance / quality / compliance overall?

## 6. Systems / roles  → `roles`
- Which platforms or teams have a clear job? (Up to 4.)
- For each, use the **same three categories** so they compare like-for-like,
  e.g. Owns / Why it matters / Vs today.

## 7. Scope  → `scope`
- What is explicitly in scope?
- What is explicitly out of scope?

## 8. Roadmap / phases / waves  → `roadmap`
- What is the high-level sequence (waves or phases)? Where are we now?
- What is the status of each (in progress, next, planned)?
- Does every phase run the same repeatable cycle? What are its steps?
- What is the outcome by the end of the period?

## 9. Milestones  → `milestones`
- What are the dated delivery checkpoints (up to 7)?
- For each: the date (or "done"/"soon"), a short label, and status
  (complete, active, upcoming).
- What is the critical path in one line?

## 10. Current state  → `current_state`
- What is complete or largely complete?
- What is stabilizing right now?
- What is at active risk or dependency? (Be honest.)
- One line describing the current mode of work.

## 11. Team & operating rhythm  → `team`
- Who owns each area? (Role → name.)
- What recurring meetings or governance keep it moving?

## 12. Next steps & help wanted  → `next_steps`
- What are the next 3-5 concrete moves, each with a timeframe?
- What do you need from leadership?
- What is the one-line definition of success?

## 13. Vision / what's next  → `closer`
- How does the model repeat and scale beyond the first delivery?
- Name the 2-3 tiers (proven → next → future) with timeframes.
- What is the single closing statement to leave on screen?

---

## Generic blocks (use as needed)
- `section` - a divider slide with a title and optional subtitle.
- `bullets` - a simple titled slide with a takeaway and a bullet list. Provide
  inline via a slide entry: `{ "block": "bullets", "content": { ... } }`.
